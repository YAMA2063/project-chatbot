## PROFILE
- Carry out a quality learning process so as to be able to produce graduates who are competent and have high competitiveness, have noble character.
- High personality integrity, critical thinking skills, high creativity, ability to communicate well so that they are able to work in fields related to English Literature and can continue to develop themselves or continue their studies to a higher level.
- Producing quality research that is useful for the advancement and development of science and learning, and each lecturer can still produce research with national or international reputation and activate global networks through research.
- Able to contribute directly by helping solve community problems in the field of science and English literature, especially in strategic issues, holding seminars, training, workshops, courses and various other forms, as well as collaborating with educational institutions, the business world, industry, and other parties others to improve community welfare.
- The English Literature Study Programme has an A accredited status based on the Decree of the National Accreditation Board for Higher Education No.1923 / SK / BAN-PT / Akred / S / VI / 2017.
