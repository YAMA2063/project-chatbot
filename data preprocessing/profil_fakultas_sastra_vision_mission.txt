## VISION
The vision of Faculty of Letters and Cultures is that in 2022, the Faculty of Letters & Cultures becomes a higher education institution in the field of literature & culture with international reputation based on excellence in holistic and integrative education, research and community service activities in order to increase national competitiveness that is able to keep up with the times by combining knowledge language, literature and culture.

## MISSIONS
- Organizing educational programs in the fields of language, literature and culture based on qualified information and communication technology (ICT) to produce excellent and globally competitive graduates.
- Creating a conducive academic atmosphere to support the implementation of the learning and teaching process that can provide inspiration and motivation for increasing research conduct of an international standard and benefit the welfare of society.
- Organizing community service activities as a manifestation of social responsibility and concern for various social problems in knowledge field with the support of the use of various supporting technologies to be able to compete in global competition.
- Organizing and developing cooperation with various institutions both domestic and abroad in order to increase the capacity of the faculty.
- Developing good governance in response to various changes that occur.
