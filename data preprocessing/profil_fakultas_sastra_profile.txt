## PROFILE
Gunadarma University's Faculty of Letters and Cultures administers three undergraduate programmes, namely the English Literature, Chinese Literature and Tourism.

- The English Literature Study Programme has an A accredited status based on the Decree of the National Accreditation Board for Higher Education No.1923/SK/BAN-PT/Akred/S/VI/2017
- The Chinese Literature Study Programme has an B accredited status based on the Decree of the National Accreditation Board for Higher Education No.3914/SK/BAN-PT/Akred/S/X/2019
- The Tourism Study Programme has an B accredited status based on the Decree of the National Accreditation Board for Higher Education No.3374/SK/BAN-PT/Akred/S/XII/2018
