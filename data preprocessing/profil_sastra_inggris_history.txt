## HISTORY
Undergraduate Programme in English Literature was established on April 3, 1996 based on Decree No: 92/DIKTI/I/Kep/1996. Since its establishment, Undergraduate Programme in English Literature has received grade A accreditation from the National Accreditation Agency for Higher Education of The Republic of Indonesia, in the years 2005, 2010 and the latest, 2017 based on the Decree of the National Accreditation Agency for Higher Education (BANPT) No.1923/SK/BAN-PT/Akred/S/VI/2017.

Undergraduate Programme in English Literature is one of thirty (30) study programmes offering English Literature Programme in Indonesia that has A accreditation at both the institutional and program level.

## HISTORICAL DEVELOPMENT
- 1996: The establishment of English Literature Undergraduate Programme
- 2002: The establishment of Master Programme for English Literature
- 2005: Undergraduate Programme in English Literature was awarded grade A from the National Accreditation Agency for Higher Education for the first time
- 2010: Undergraduate Programme in English Literature awarded grade A from the National Accreditation Agency for Higher Education for the second time
- 2016: Faculty of Letters transformed into Faculty of Letters and Cultures with an addition of two programmes; Chinese Literature Programme and Tourism Programme
- 2017: Undergraduate Programme in English Literature awarded grade A from the National Accreditation Agency for Higher Education of The Republic of Indonesia for the third time
