## FOREWORD
Gunadarma University has a long and illustrious history, as well as the Faculty of Letters and Cultures which was established on April 3, 1996. One of the faculty's renowned study programme is the English Literature Programme. The English Literature Programme envisions to have a significant contribution in increasing the nation's competitiveness in the field of English Literature, through the combination of the knowledge and application of English literature with technology.
