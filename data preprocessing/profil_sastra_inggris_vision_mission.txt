## VISION
In 2022, English Literature Study Program become a Study Program with an international reputation based on excellence in educational, research and community service activities that are holistic and integrative in order to increase the competitiveness of the nation that is able to keep up with the times by combining language, literature and culture.

## MISSIONS
- Organizing education and training programs to produce graduates who have high competence and competitiveness in the application of English literature in various aspects of life, especially in information technology-based journalism and public relations applications.
- Creating a conducive academic atmosphere and supporting research activities of an international standard and beneficial to human welfare.
- Organizing community service activities as a form of institutional social responsibility.
- Organizing and developing cooperation networks with various institutions, both at home and abroad as an effort to improve the quality of study programs.
